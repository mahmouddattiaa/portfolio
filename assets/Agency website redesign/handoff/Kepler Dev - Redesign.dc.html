How the studio works

Founder-led, and explicit about it.

Kepler Dev is a founder-led product studio. I lead product strategy, architecture, and delivery, and bring in vetted specialists when a project needs additional design or engineering capacity. You are told who is working on what.

The studio does not claim a permanent team it does not have, present prototypes as production client work, or promise capacity it cannot reliably deliver. That constraint is the reason the pricing, scope, and proof on this site are written the way they are.
