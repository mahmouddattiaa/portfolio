Last updated: October 24, 2023. At FitForge, we value your data as much as your performance. This document outlines how we protect your biological and digital blueprint.

01

Data Collection

We collect information that helps us optimize your training experience. This includes biometric data such as heart rate, movement velocity, and caloric expenditure during your sessions.

Additionally, we process technical identifiers including device IP addresses, operating system versions, and usage logs to ensure the Forge remains stable under heavy load.

Crucial Note

"FitForge never sells your personal biometric data to third-party advertisers. Your progress is your own."

02

Your Rights

You maintain full sovereignty over your data. Under global protection regulations, you have the right to:

  • visibility
    Access & Portability Request a complete export of your workout history and biometric records at any time.
  • delete_forever
    Right to Erasure Permanently wipe your profile and all associated performance data from our servers.
  • edit_note
    Rectification Correct inaccurate measurements or profile details to ensure your training metrics remain precise.
03

Security

Our security infrastructure is built like a fortress. We utilize AES-256 encryption for all data at rest and TLS 1.3 for all data in transit.
