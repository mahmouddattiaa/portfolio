HOW WE WORK

Three engagements.

Each one is paid, each one has a defined end, and each one is useful on its own. They compose in order, but none of them obliges you to the next.

Product Blueprint

01
FOR
A high-stakes workflow that needs clarity before anyone writes code.
YOU GET
A decision-ready direction: the scope, the technical plan, the risks named out loud, and an estimate you can hold us to.
IT STOPS AT
A foundation, not a disguised build. No production code ships in a Blueprint.

Then → Most continue into a Launch Sprint. Yours does not have to; the plan is yours either way.

Launch Sprint

02
FOR
A defined product or workflow that is ready to become a working release.
YOU GET
A usable product in production, the essentials connected, and the decisions behind it written down rather than carried in someone's head.
IT STOPS AT
The agreed outcome, not an open-ended feature list. New scope is a new agreement, said plainly.

Then → Hand it to your own team with the handover done properly, or continue into Product Care.

Product Care

03
FOR
A live product that needs deliberate improvement — not a help desk.
YOU GET
Agreed capacity each month for prioritised fixes, refinements and the next increment, against a backlog you own.
IT STOPS AT
The priorities you set. It is not an open retainer, and it is not on-call cover.

Then → It runs for as long as it earns its place, and ends cleanly when it stops earning it.
