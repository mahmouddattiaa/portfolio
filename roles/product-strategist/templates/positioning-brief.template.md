## 2. Target Buyer Profiles (ICPs)

### Primary Buyer: GCC & MENA Operations Leaders & Business Owners
- **Industries:** Fuel/energy retail, loyalty networks, healthcare clinics, hospitality/property management, field services, and regional EdTech.
- **Triggering Pain:** Operations depend on disconnected WhatsApp groups, spreadsheets, manual reconciliations, or brittle legacy apps that fail on poor connections.
- **What Convinces Them:** Arabic-first RTL + English bilingual fluency, append-only financial/points ledgers, offline-capable station/field apps, and UK data governance.

### Secondary Buyer: International Product Founders & Technical Leads (CTOs)
- **Triggering Pain:** Need a focused, production-grade v1 web/mobile release or complex native OS/hardware integration (`Kotlin`, `Swift`, `Java` POS, `WireGuard`, `OpenAPI`) without hiring 4 separate contractors.
- **What Convinces Them:** Shipped Google Play / Azure production proof, contract-first CI drift detection, and full source-code handover.

## 4. Four Commercial Engagement Offers (`How We Work`)

| Offer Name | Best Fit | Duration & Starting Range | Concrete Deliverables | Explicit Boundary | Next Step |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kepler Product Blueprint** | High-stakes workflow or product idea needing technical clarity before quoting a build. | 5–10 business days (\$350–\$1,200) | Discovery workshop, workflow map, OpenAPI/system architecture, v1 scope with exclusions, milestone plan & build estimate. | Foundation only; no production code ships in a Blueprint. | Client owns the Blueprint outright; continue into Launch Sprint or Operations Platform. |
| **Kepler Launch Sprint** | Validated product or single workflow ready to become a live v1 release. | 6–10 weeks (\$3,000–\$8,000) | One primary web or mobile surface, core user journey, auth, backend API/DB, admin view, and production deployment. | Agreed v1 scope; new workflows require a separate phase. | Full code handover or transition into Product Care. |
| **Kepler Operations Platform** | Multi-role operational business digitizing customer, staff, and head-office workflows. | 8–16+ weeks phased (\$6,000–\$15,000+ Phase 1) | Connected customer app, staff/POS app, and management dashboard under one API contract and ledger. | Phased milestone delivery with explicit acceptance gates. | Expand into Phase 2 integrations or ongoing Product Care. |
| **Kepler Product Care** | Live production system needing deliberate monthly engineering and reliability. | Monthly (\$400–\$1,500+/mo) | Agreed monthly engineering capacity for prioritized roadmap increments, security updates, and release verification. | Prioritized backlog execution, not 24/7 unscheduled help-desk cover. | Runs monthly for as long as it delivers value. |
