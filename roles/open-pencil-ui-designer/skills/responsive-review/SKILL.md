# Screens and visual review

Condensed adaptation of [Seth Hobson responsive design](https://github.com/wshobson/agents/blob/main/plugins/ui-design/skills/responsive-design/SKILL.md), consulted 2026-11-01; MIT notice in `../LICENSES.md`.

Use the audited Role 1 layout and Role 2A visual system. Full homepage frames show all six sections and complete locked content in its specified visible/disclosed states. Include expanded-state examples so hidden copy remains designed, not merely mentioned.

Build 1440px desktop, 390px mobile and a 320px reflow sample. Full pages grow naturally. Separate first-view frames at 1440x900 and 390x844 show actual fold boundaries; record whether H1, availability, primary CTA and all four proof pills fit. Do not extend the viewport frame or shrink fonts to claim a pass.

Use 44px minimum controls, fluid auto-layout, safe gutters and readable full headings. Portrait screenshot proportions and long desktop captures stay intact. Separate compact card previews from full gallery geometry. Build six project-specific desktop detail dialogs and six mobile detail pages, not generic empty templates.

Read `../../VISUAL-INSPECTION.md`. Inspect a screenshot immediately after each completed desktop/mobile section or page, before starting the next. Open the image at a readable size and check full text, crops, fonts, spacing, overlaps, expanded content and focus/error states. Fix visible problems, then capture and inspect again. Recheck completed work and affected instances after changes. Canvas lint and overlap checks support this loop; they do not replace image inspection. Record image paths, issues, fixes and final results in the handoff.

The canvas cannot prove browser history, DOM accessibility, keyboard focus trapping or real responsive reflow. Record those as future implementation checks. No browser PASS without an actual browser test.
