## 1. Purpose of this document

This document is the working source of truth for building Kepler Dev. It records the foundational decisions, separates confirmed facts from assumptions, and defines the path through validation, offer construction, sales, and controlled market entry.

Kepler Dev is not intended to remain a branded freelance practice. It will begin as a founder-led boutique product studio, develop repeatable sales and delivery systems, expand into multiple delivery teams when revenue supports them, and use retained profit to launch software products owned by the company.

---

## 2. Executive summary

Kepler Dev will help nontechnical founders and growing businesses turn validated ideas and operational problems into launch-ready software. Its initial services will be complete MVP development, mobile and web applications, business systems, dashboards, integrations, deployment, and ongoing product support.

The company will use two long-term growth engines:

1. **Kepler Studio:** Client services that generate cash flow, relationships, proof, and employment.
2. **Kepler Labs:** Internally owned software products that can create recurring and scalable revenue.

The company will operate honestly as a founder-led studio. It may assemble vetted specialists for projects, but it will not invent employees, clients, results, capacity, or testimonials.

# Stage 1 — Agency Foundation

By August 2027, Kepler Dev aims to be a growing software company serving GCC and international clients through multiple specialized delivery teams. Its client-services division will provide reliable product development and create high-quality employment opportunities, while its product division begins launching and growing software products owned by Kepler Dev.

### Founder ambitions

- Win valuable contracts from GCC and international clients.
- Employ and develop capable people in Egypt.
- Benefit from international revenue while building an Egyptian delivery base.
- Move progressively from implementation into product direction, sales, leadership, and capital allocation.

| Business stage | Mahmoud's primary responsibilities |
| --- | --- |

## 4. Business model

### Starting model

Kepler Dev will launch as a **founder-led boutique product studio designed for future scale**.

Boutique describes the starting operating stage, not the final ambition. Kepler Dev will build scalable processes from the beginning but will not activate permanent teams before demand and cash flow justify them.

### Long-term company architecture

#### Kepler Studio

The client-services engine will provide:

- Product discovery and technical scoping
- Launch-ready MVP development
- Mobile and web application development
- Business systems and operational dashboards
- APIs, third-party integrations, payments, and deployment
- Maintenance and continuous product-development retainers

#### Kepler Labs

The owned-product engine will eventually manage:

- Internally developed mobile and web products
- Product validation and controlled launches
- Monetization experiments
- Reusable intellectual property where contractually permitted
- Potential standalone SaaS products or spin-offs

Kepler Studio and Kepler Labs will remain parts of one brand initially. A separate public Labs identity is unnecessary until an owned product approaches launch.

## 5. Market positioning

### Working identity

> Kepler Dev is a founder-led product studio that turns validated ideas and operational problems into launch-ready web and mobile products.

### Primary buyer

Nontechnical founders with validated product ideas who need one accountable partner to plan, build, deploy, and support an MVP.

### Primary technical/product sweet spot

Operations-heavy software involving areas such as:

- Multiple user roles
- Administrative dashboards
- Payments or POS integrations
- Loyalty systems
- Field workers or distributed operations
- Offline-first workflows
- Automation and AI-assisted features
- Mobile applications connected to business backends

### Secondary buyer

Growing GCC businesses that need to replace manual workflows or build customer- and employee-facing digital systems.

### Market order

1. GCC direct clients for long-term, relationship-driven opportunities.
2. Upwork and international clients for faster market feedback, contracts, reviews, and proof.
3. Selective Egyptian clients when the project is profitable, strategically valuable, or referral-driven.

Egyptian clients may receive smaller or phased packages. Kepler Dev will change scope and packaging rather than provide the same work at an unsustainable price.

## 7. Brand and communication

### Brand character

> The calm technical product partner who tells founders what they need to hear, explains why, and takes responsibility for delivery.

### Communication style

Kepler Dev will combine:

1. **Direct and concise:** Lead with the recommendation or decision.
2. **Advisory and thorough:** Explain reasoning, risks, and trade-offs.
3. **Warm and founder-friendly:** Make nontechnical buyers feel respected and supported.
4. **Technically credible:** Use engineering detail when it improves a decision or the buyer requests it.

### Communication rules

- Lead with business outcomes.
- Use plain language.
- Make clear recommendations.
- Explain cost, risk, and trade-offs honestly.
- Challenge unnecessary features respectfully.
- Stay calm and transparent when problems occur.
- Never exaggerate results, delivery speed, or team size.
- Avoid empty claims such as “world-class,” “revolutionary,” and “cutting-edge solutions.”

### Founder-led disclosure

Recommended wording:

> Kepler Dev is a founder-led product studio. Mahmoud leads product strategy, architecture, and delivery, bringing in vetted specialists when a project requires additional design or engineering capacity.

Using “we” as the company voice is acceptable as long as Kepler Dev does not falsely imply a permanent staff or capacity it does not possess.

## 10. Financial foundation

### Founder capital

- Total savings ceiling: approximately **$2,000**.
- Initial 90-day spending authority: **no more than $500**.
- Protected personal reserve: **at least $1,500**.
- The $2,000 ceiling is not a spending target.

### Preliminary 90-day allocation

| Category | Maximum |
| --- | ---: |
| Sales and outreach | $150 |
| Website, email, and infrastructure | $100 |
| Case-study or design support | $100 |
| Legal/accounting consultation after revenue begins | $100 |
| Contingency | $50 |

### Revenue milestones

| Average monthly revenue | Meaning |
| ---: | --- |
| $1,000 | Initial market proof |
| $2,000 | First major milestone and founder-sustaining studio |
| $5,000 | Stable boutique agency |
| $10,000 | Scaling-ready agency |
| $15,000+ | Potential multi-team operation |

These are planning milestones, not guaranteed outcomes. Revenue must not be confused with profit or founder income.

### Cash-flow principles

- Savings fund validation and essential setup.
- Client deposits fund client delivery.
- Retained profit funds growth and owned products.
- Client deposits must not be diverted into speculative product development.
- Contractors are engaged only when signed work and cleared payments cover their cost.
- Full-time hiring requires stable revenue, upcoming contracted work, documented processes, and an operating reserve.

### Preliminary client payment structure

- 40% before work begins
- 30% after an agreed milestone
- 30% before final production handover

Larger projects may use paid weekly or biweekly milestones. The commercial structure will be finalized during offer design.

### Legal and tax warning

The appropriate Egyptian legal form, tax obligations, invoicing requirements, employment obligations, and treatment of foreign-currency revenue remain unresolved. Advice from a qualified Egyptian accountant or lawyer will be required before formal operations and hiring.

## 11. Hiring policy

Kepler Dev will start solo and build a vetted contractor network before creating permanent payroll.

A person is hired or contracted only when:

- Signed work requires their skill.
- Project economics cover their full cost.
- Their output can be reviewed and measured.
- Delivery standards and responsibilities are documented.
- Revenue is stable rather than based on one exceptional month.
- The agency retains enough cash to withstand delayed payments.
- Delegating the role allows Mahmoud to perform higher-value work.

Likely early delegation areas are UI/UX design, QA, specialist engineering, overflow development, bookkeeping, and operational support. Founder-led sales should continue until a repeatable acquisition system exists.

## 13. Integrity and operating principles

Kepler Dev will:

- Present itself professionally from the beginning.
- Build scalable systems before hiring multiple teams.
- Be explicit about its founder-led structure.
- Promise only the capacity it can reliably deliver.
- Use truthful case-study statuses and measurable evidence.
- Pay contributors fairly and avoid building growth on hidden unpaid labor.
- Protect client deposits and intellectual property.
- Prefer long-term trust over short-term appearance.

Kepler Dev will not:

- Invent employees, offices, clients, testimonials, revenue, or results.
- Claim team ownership of work Mahmoud did not perform.
- Present academic or prototype work as production client work.
- Hire permanent staff based on speculative future contracts.
- allow client work, product experiments, and personal finances to become indistinguishable.

# Stage 2 — Market Validation, Proof, and Offer Construction

## 16. Stage 2 objective

Stage 2 will convert the strategic foundation into a business that can credibly approach and close clients.

The stage answers four questions:

1. Which previous work provides the strongest, safest proof?
2. Which buyer and problem produce the clearest commercial opportunity?
3. What exactly can a client purchase from Kepler Dev?
4. Can the offer generate qualified conversations before major spending?

---

## 17. Stage 2 workstreams

### Workstream A — Project and proof inventory

For every meaningful project, record:

- Classification: client, employer, university, internal, or owned product
- Production status
- Target user and problem
- Mahmoud's exact contribution
- Team context
- Technical scope
- Business or user outcome
- Available evidence
- Client-name and screenshot permissions
- Public, anonymized, or private status

**Deliverables:**

- Master project inventory
- Proof-strength scoring matrix
- Three to five shortlisted agency case studies
- Personal-portfolio project shortlist
- Confidential-project register
- List of missing screenshots, metrics, testimonials, and permissions

### Workstream B — Buyer and market validation

Research and test:

- Upwork demand for MVPs, operational systems, and mobile/business applications
- GCC demand from founders and growing businesses
- Common budgets, timelines, requested features, and objections
- Competing studios' positioning and proof
- Words buyers use when describing their problems
- Differences between weak leads and qualified buyers

Validation should use observable evidence such as real job posts, prospect conversations, proposal responses, and competitor offers.

**Deliverables:**

- Primary ideal-client profile
- Secondary ideal-client profile
- Buyer qualification and rejection criteria
- Competitor and positioning map
- Market-language library
- Evidence-backed niche recommendation

### Workstream C — Offer architecture

Design the initial productized offers:

1. Paid Product Discovery Sprint
2. Focused Launch-Ready MVP
3. Business Operations System
4. Continuous Product Support

Each offer must specify:

- Intended buyer
- Starting problem
- Promised outcome
- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Pricing method and starting range
- Milestones and payment terms
- Client responsibilities
- Revision policy
- Included and excluded work
- Completion and acceptance criteria
- Upsell or continuation path

**Deliverables:**

- Four offer cards
- Scope-boundary checklist
- Estimation framework
- Pricing and margin calculator
- Standard payment schedule

### Workstream D — Sales foundation

Build the smallest functioning sales system:

- Upwork profile positioning
- Proposal structure
- GCC outreach message
- Qualification form
- Discovery-call agenda
- Follow-up sequence
- Proposal template
- Simple CRM stages
- Weekly sales scorecard

**Deliverables:**

- Channel-specific messaging
- Qualification checklist
- Discovery-call script
- Proposal template
- Follow-up templates
- Lead-tracking system

## 18. Recommended Stage 2 sequence

### Week 1 — Evidence and project inventory

- Receive the project Markdown files.
- Normalize every project into one inventory.
- Identify confidentiality gaps.
- Score projects for commercial relevance, credibility, visual quality, and evidence.
- Select the best case-study candidates.

### Week 2 — Market and buyer validation

- Review real opportunities across Upwork, GCC, and selective Egyptian channels.
- Study comparable agencies and freelancers.
- Identify repeating buyer problems and budget signals.
- Test whether the initial founder/MVP positioning is sufficiently sharp.
- Finalize qualification and rejection criteria.

### Week 3 — Offer and pricing design

- Define the Discovery Sprint.
- Define the flagship MVP offer.
- Define the business-system offer.
- Define the ongoing support offer.
- Build scope, pricing, margin, and payment rules.

### Week 4 — Sales-system preparation and soft launch

- Rewrite the Upwork positioning.
- Create proposal and discovery templates.
- Build the first lead list.
- Send a controlled batch of high-quality proposals and outreach.
- Record responses and revise the offer language.

The website redesign should begin only after Stage 2 produces validated positioning, case-study priorities, and offer language.

- Every major project has been classified.
- Three to five defensible case studies have been selected.

# Stage 3 — Sales Foundation and Controlled Launch

## 21. Stage 3 objective

Stage 3 converts the market position and productized offers into a repeatable client-acquisition process. It begins before Stage 2 field validation is formally complete because live sales activity is the mechanism that will validate the buyer, message, scope, and pricing.

The complete Stage 3 operating system is defined in `Kepler-Dev-Sales-Foundation.md`.

### Primary workstreams

1. Optimize Mahmoud's founder-led Upwork profile and proof.
2. Send selective proposals to high-fit international opportunities.
3. Build and contact a researched GCC target-account list.
4. Use a consistent qualification and discovery process.
5. Convert uncertain opportunities into paid Product Blueprints.
6. Track responses, objections, pipeline value, and collected revenue.
7. Revise the offer using live evidence.

### Controlled-launch targets

- 20 selective Upwork proposals.
- 30 researched GCC accounts.
- 15 personalized GCC messages.
- 10 warm-introduction requests.
- Five qualified discovery conversations.
- Two paid Blueprint proposals.
- At least one build proposal.

### Portfolio rebuild gate and schedule

Portfolio preparation begins in parallel with the controlled launch; the final message should not be designed in isolation from market feedback.

- **8–16 August 2026:** collect repository access, brand assets, case-study facts, screenshot requirements, permissions, and the first outreach responses.
- **After 5 Upwork proposals and 5 GCC messages, targeted for 17–20 August:** begin the agency portfolio information architecture, homepage positioning, service pages, and case-study narrative structure.
- **21–30 August:** design and implement the new agency-facing experience using approved assets and honest placeholders where evidence is still pending.
- **31 August–4 September:** responsive QA, content review, analytics/contact-flow checks, deployment, and domain verification.

The personal portfolio may remain accessible under a dedicated route such as `/mahmoud` or `/founder`. The final route and repository architecture will be decided after the existing codebase is inspected.

The website does not need to wait for all 30-day sales targets, but its copy must remain changeable until the controlled launch produces meaningful buyer feedback.

### Completion condition

Stage 3 is complete only after the sales system has been used with real prospects. Templates alone do not constitute validation.
